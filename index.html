About

I'm finishing an M.S. in Applied AI at Northeastern, after a B.S. in Electrical Engineering & Computer Science at IISER Bhopal. My focus sits across multi-agent reinforcement learning, LLM-based pipelines, and autonomous robotics.

I care more about the parts that don't demo well — evals, failure modes, deployment constraints — than about a clean one-off result. Understanding why an agent breaks, and building the scaffold that stops it, is the work I actually enjoy.

Right now that means AgentSWE: a scaffold aimed at the most common failure modes on SWE-bench Pro, built around a simple thesis — better scaffolding on a cheaper model (Claude Haiku) can close a meaningful share of the gap to expensive-model baselines. Earlier work includes two industry-sponsored projects with OpenEye Scientific Software and Cadence, and a placement at the 2026 Red Hat Hackathon.
